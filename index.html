Phenomenology of Neutrino and WIMP Dark Matter based on Lepton Flavor Symmetry

I'm interested in Phenomenology beyond the standard model, in particular neutrinos and dark matter.

  • Research on WIMP Dark Matter Based on a Z${_4}$ Flavor Symmetry: In this work, I studied the possibility that the origin of dark matter stability and the very small scattering cross section with nucleons comes from its flavor structure.
  • Neutrino Study Based on a U(1)$_{L_\mu -L_\tau}^{}$ Gauge Model: In this work, I studied a U(1)$_{L_\mu -L_\tau}^{}$ gauge symmetric model, which has been widely discussed as a framework to explain both the muon g−2 anomaly and neutrino oscillations and masses.
  • Exploration of New Physics Models Using Machine Learning: In these works, I introduced reinforcement learning, a type of machine learning, as a new exploration method to construct theoretical models that address quark and lepton flavor structures and cosmological problems.
