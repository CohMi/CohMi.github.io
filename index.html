Research Interests

I am interested in phenomenology beyond the Standard Model, particularly focusing on neutrinos and dark matter.

Phenomenology of Neutrino and WIMP Dark Matter based on Lepton Flavor Symmetry

  • Research on WIMP Dark Matter Based on a $Z_4$ Flavor Symmetry: I studied the possibility that the origin of dark matter stability and the very small scattering cross section with nucleons comes from its flavor structure.
  • Neutrino Study Based on a $U(1)_{L_\mu -L_\tau}$ Gauge Model: I investigated a $U(1)_{L_\mu -L_\tau}$ gauge symmetric model, widely discussed as a framework to explain both the muon $g-2$ anomaly and neutrino oscillations/masses.
  • Exploration of New Physics Models Using Machine Learning: I introduced reinforcement learning as a new exploration method to construct theoretical models addressing quark/lepton flavor structures and cosmological problems.
