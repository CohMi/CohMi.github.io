Research

Dark matter, neutrinos & flavor

I connect theoretical model building with experimental constraints and numerical methods, including tests at future experiments such as HK, FCC, and DUNE.

Dark matter

I explore how lepton flavor symmetry stabilizes WIMP dark matter and suppresses its interactions with ordinary matter, while leaving signatures accessible to future direct-detection experiments.

Related publication [1] ↓

Neutrinos & flavor symmetry

I study neutrino masses and mixing in gauged $U(1)_{L_\mu-L_\tau}$ models, and test neutrino mass textures against oscillation, cosmological, and other experimental constraints.

Related publications [3] [5]

Machine learning for model building

I use reinforcement learning to search for flavor charge assignments that reproduce quark and lepton masses and mixing, including axion models that connect flavor physics to the strong-CP problem.
